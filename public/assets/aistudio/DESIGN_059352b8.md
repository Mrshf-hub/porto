# Design System: Dark Cinematic Game-Menu Editorial UI (Marsha Yoseline Fau 2026)

## Brand Identity & Personality
- **Aesthetic:** Dark Cinematic, Game-Menu UI, Creative Editorial Showcase, High-Contrast Atmospheric Glow.
- **Audience:** Creative agencies, HR recruiters, event committees, entertainment & tech clients.
- **Mood:** Immersive, bold, disciplined, premium tech & cinematic storytelling.

---

## 1. Color Palette & Tokens

### Background & Void
- `--bg-void`: `#05080D` (Primary background deep black void)
- `--bg-deep`: `#07121E` (Secondary dark navy background)
- `--bg-card`: `rgba(12, 26, 42, 0.65)` (Glassmorphism card surface)
- `--bg-overlay`: `rgba(5, 8, 13, 0.85)` (Backdrop & modal overlay)

### Atmospheric Glow & Light Effects
- `--glow-teal`: `rgba(18, 78, 114, 0.45)` (Radial lighting at top-right & center edges)
- `--glow-cyan`: `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)` (Accent highlights, focus glow)
- `--glow-crimson`: `rgba(158, 19, 36, 0.35)` (Hero pill & action accent glow)

### Primary & Accent Colors
- `--accent-crimson`: `#9E1324` (Hero capsule pill, metric highlight badges)
- `--accent-crimson-bright`: `#E11D48` (Active hover indicators, arrows `▸ ◂`)
- `--accent-cyan`: `#7DD3FC` (Subtitles, metadata tags, links, focus borders)
- `--accent-gold`: `#F59E0B` (Floating embers & subtle warm highlights)

### Neutral & Text Ink
- `--ink-primary`: `#F4F7FB` (Display headings, pure high-contrast text)
- `--ink-soft`: `rgba(244, 247, 251, 0.70)` (Body paragraphs, secondary descriptions)
- `--ink-muted`: `rgba(244, 247, 251, 0.40)` (Timestamps, metadata, captions, footer)
- `--border-subtle`: `rgba(220, 232, 245, 0.14)` (Subtle glass container outlines)
- `--border-focus`: `rgba(125, 211, 252, 0.50)` (Hover state for cards & interactive pills)

### Pill Component Tokens
- `--pill-bg`: `#DCE8F5` (High-contrast active navigation pill background)
- `--pill-ink`: `#060A10` (Dark ink text on active navigation pill)
- `--pill-inactive-bg`: `rgba(12, 26, 42, 0.55)` (Glass inactive pill surface)

## 2. Typography

### Font Families
- **Display Font:** `'Anton', sans-serif`
  - Style: Ultra-bold condensed display, uppercase, heavy geometric punch.
  - Usage: Main section headlines (`PORTFOLIO`, `ABOUT ME`, `RECAP PROJECT`, `THANK YOU`), brand statement.
  - Tracking: `0.02em` – `0.04em`.
  - Line-height: `0.92` – `1.0`.

- **Numeral & Label Font:** `'Barlow Condensed', sans-serif`
  - Weights: `300` (Light), `600` (Semi-bold), `700` (Bold).
  - Usage: Year numbers (`2026`), category badges, timeline ranges (`2024 – 2026`), metric tags.
  - Tracking: `0.06em` – `0.15em` uppercase.

- **Body & Interface Font:** `'Plus Jakarta Sans', sans-serif`
  - Weights: `400` (Regular), `500` (Medium), `600` (Semi-bold), `700` (Bold).
  - Usage: Body copy, navigation pill labels, descriptions, experience & education items.
  - Line-height: `1.6` – `1.7`.

### Type Scale
- **Display Hero:** `clamp(56px, 8.5vw, 130px)` / Line-height `0.92` (Anton)
- **Section Heading (H1):** `clamp(36px, 5.5vw, 64px)` (Anton)
- **Sub-heading (H2):** `clamp(24px, 3.5vw, 32px)` (Anton)
- **Card Title (H3):** `20px – 24px` (Anton / Barlow Condensed 700)
- **Body Large:** `16px` / Line-height `1.6`
- **Body Regular:** `13.5px – 14px` / Line-height `1.7` (Plus Jakarta Sans)
- **Caption / Badge:** `10px – 11px` / Weight `700` / Letter-spacing `0.14em`

## 3. Spacing, Layout & Grid Systems

### Spacing Scale
- `space-xs`: `4px`
- `space-sm`: `8px`
- `space-md`: `16px`
- `space-lg`: `24px`
- `space-xl`: `36px`
- `space-2xl`: `54px`
- `space-3xl`: `80px` – `110px` (Section padding vertical)

### Container & Layout Rules
- **Viewport Sections:** Full-screen snap feeling with `min-height: 100svh`, padded at `110px 6vw 90px`.
- **Max Content Width:** `1200px` centered.
- **Bento Grid:** 12-column layout (`repeat(12, 1fr)`) with `18px` gaps. Cards span `span-6`, `span-5`, `span-4`, or `span-3` with auto-row minimum `210px`.
- **Two-Column Split Layouts:** `0.85fr 1.15fr` (Profile) or `1.05fr 0.95fr` (Contact & Thank You).

## 4. UI Components & Patterns

### 1. Top Floating Pill Navigation
- Centered sticky row (`top: 20px`, `z-index: 50`).
- Container: Flex row with `gap: 6px`, subtle glass background (`rgba(7, 18, 30, 0.7)`), backdrop-filter blur `16px`, border-radius `999px`, border `1px solid var(--border-subtle)`.
- Active Pill: Background `var(--pill-bg)` (`#DCE8F5`), text `var(--pill-ink)` (`#060A10`), padding `7px 20px`, shadow `0 0 20px rgba(220, 232, 245, 0.3)`.
- Inactive Pill: Color `var(--ink-soft)`, transparent background, hover color `#fff`.

### 2. Hero Crimson Capsule Frame
- Anchored flush to the left screen border (`border-radius: 0 999px 999px 0`).
- Background: Linear gradient (`135deg, #6E0D19, #9E1324`).
- Shadow: `0 25px 60px rgba(158, 19, 36, 0.35)`.
- Photo inside masked and positioned top-center with warm rim drop-shadow.

### 3. Bento Project Cards
- Surface: Glassmorphic dark card (`background: rgba(12, 26, 42, 0.55)`), border `1px solid var(--border-subtle)`, border-radius `14px`.
- Top-left Badge: Pill shape, `background: rgba(158, 19, 36, 0.9)`, color `#fff`, `font-size: 10px`, weight `700`, uppercase.
- Bottom Content Overlay: Dark linear gradient upwards (`rgba(5,8,13,0.95)` to transparent).
- Hover Interaction: `transform: translateY(-4px)`, border color `rgba(125, 211, 252, 0.5)`, box-shadow `0 18px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.55)`, internal thumbnail image zoom `scale(1.05)`.

### 4. Software Skill Badges
- Size: `48x48px` rounded-square tiles (`border-radius: 12px`).
- Styling: Deep brand-tinted gradient background, crisp 2-letter monogram or SVG glyph, box-shadow `0 8px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.4)`.
- Hover: `translateY(-4px) scale(1.08)` + floating informative tooltip.

### 5. Game-Menu Vertical Action List
- Centered stack with items spaced `14px`.
- Typography: Barlow Condensed / Plus Jakarta Sans bold uppercase with wide letter-spacing (`0.24em`).
- Hover State: Text turns pure white, subtle glow background, caret symbols `▸` and `◂` appear on sides in crimson bright.

---

## 5. Atmosphere & Motion Guidelines
- **Atmospheric Noise:** Fine grain overlay (`opacity: 0.035`) over deep gradient radial meshes to eliminate color banding.
- **Ember Particle Animation:** Subtle floating glowing particles ascending slowly from behind portrait stage.
- **Transitions:** Standard easing `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` over `0.3s` – `0.4s` for hover, `0.8s` for viewport scroll reveals.
- **Accessibility:** Full support for `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`, disabling continuous particle floating and scaling animations while preserving crisp interactive state transitions.
